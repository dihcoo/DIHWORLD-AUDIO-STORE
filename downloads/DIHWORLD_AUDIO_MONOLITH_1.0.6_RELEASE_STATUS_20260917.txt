DIHWorld Audio — MONOLITH 1.0.6 Release Status
Date: 2026-09-17
Status: Apple-notarized, stapled, installed for Logic, and AU-validated.

MONOLITH now isolates DSP and preset transitions to the main output bus so external sidechain topology cannot contaminate, multiply, or accelerate audible program material. The sidechain remains detector-only, Gate is a true dry bypass, mono-to-stereo input is copied correctly, and Gain Match uses true RMS with safe reset behavior.

Universal AU, VST3, and standalone builds passed the DIH Engine contract. The signed AU installed in Logic reports 1.0.6 and passed auval, including mono, stereo, mono-to-stereo, render, and parameter tests. The signed PKG and DIH-image DMG are Apple-notarized and stapled.

SILVER is the neutral M50 reference. GOLD is the color processor.
