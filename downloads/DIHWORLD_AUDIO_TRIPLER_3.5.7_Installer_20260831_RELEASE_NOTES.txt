DIHWorld Audio TRIPLER 3.5.7
Mac Release · 2026-08-31

TRIPLER 3.5.7 restores the sacred 3.5.7 version while correcting the internal three-voice balance across every factory preset.

Voices 2 and 3 are now calibrated to sit evenly beside the lead voice. The factory side-voice levels and shared harmony blend were raised together so the harmony remains present without changing the master output balance.

The individual installer includes universal Mac AU and VST3 formats. Delivery is through protected customer access.
