DIHWorld Audio — MARIANA 1.0.3 Release Status
Date: 2026-09-17
Status: Apple-notarized, stapled, installed for Logic, and AU-validated.

Gain Match now compares true dry-input RMS with processed-output RMS and resets safely. The canonical DIHWorld Audio URL is restored.

The signed PKG and DIH-image DMG are Apple-notarized and stapled.
