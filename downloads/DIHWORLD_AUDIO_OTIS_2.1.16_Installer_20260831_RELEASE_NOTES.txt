DIHWorld Audio OTIS 2.1.16
Mac Release · 2026-08-31

OTIS 2.1.16 calibrates the neutral default output level to the DIHWorld bus reference. The default program is now level-matched to the reference at -23.9 LUFS; Gain Match remains unchanged and available for user-controlled input/output matching.

The individual installer includes the universal Mac AU, VST3, and standalone application. Delivery is through protected customer access.
