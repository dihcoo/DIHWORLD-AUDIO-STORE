DIHWorld Audio Complete Installer
Mac Release · 2026-08-31

The Complete installer carries the current 42-product DIHWorld Audio collection, including OTIS 2.1.16 and TRIPLER 3.5.7.

OTIS 2.1.16 calibrates its neutral default output to the DIHWorld bus reference while preserving the Gain Match feature. TRIPLER 3.5.7 corrects Voices 2 and 3 across every factory preset. Delivery is through protected customer access.
