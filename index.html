About Us

DIHWorld Audio

DIHWorld Audio builds connected production systems around the Imhotep 369 Engine—our Triadic Harmonic Lattice—and Ma’at 357 / MomentumBend. Phi-derived control, a Phi/2 canonical ceiling, and structured signal flow preserve movement, balance, and musical intent without brickwall limiting.

Our perspective was shaped by decades of listening, composition, production, and engineering—especially the relationship between space, impact, frequency, and the human ear. DIHWorld exists to turn that patient psycho-acoustic study into tools that keep creative control in the hands of the artist: clear enough to trust, responsive enough to perform with, and built to give every mix a fuller sense of dimension.
