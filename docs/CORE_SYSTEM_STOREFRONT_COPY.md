### SacredVerb

Sacred Space. Emotional Decay. Living Reverb.

SacredVerb is an expressive reverb system designed to create emotionally responsive space.

Unlike static algorithmic reverbs, SacredVerb is tuned for musical decay behavior, harmonic movement, and evolving tail structure.

Key features:

- Emotion-driven reverb engine
- Harmonic decay shaping
- Smooth, musical tail evolution
- Designed for vocals, keys, pads, and melodic sources
- Clean mix integration with controlled density

Best used for:

- Vocals that need emotional depth
- Pads and ambient textures
- Cinematic sound design
- Lead instruments

### Cathedral

Architectural Space Modeling Reverb.

Cathedral is a structured reverb engine based on architectural acoustic design.

Where SacredVerb is emotional, Cathedral is physical space logic. It simulates dimensional environments with clarity, reflection structure, and controlled diffusion.

Key features:

- Architectural reverb modeling
- Defined early reflections and spatial depth
- Clear mix placement control
- Designed for realism and width control
- Stable low-end response without wash buildup

Best used for:

- Drums in real spaces
- Orchestral instruments
- Ambient room creation
- Natural vocal placement

Sound philosophy: Space is geometry you can hear.

### DEBO

Dynamic Ceiling Control and Mix Protection System.

DEBO is your mix safety engine.

It is designed to control peaks, stabilize dynamics, and enforce clean output behavior without destroying musical movement. It is not a character limiter. It is a precision ceiling tool.

Key features:

- Transparent limiting behavior
- Controlled gain structure management
- Clean transient preservation
- Mix bus safety control
- Designed for mastering and final stages

Best used for:

- Master bus control
- Drum bus protection
- Vocal peak control
- Final mix safety layer

Sound philosophy: Control without punishment.

Actions: Buy DEBO / Download Demo

### DrumKrushGlue

Parallel Drum Energy Engine.

DrumKrushGlue is a rhythmic density processor designed to unify drums into a single powerful force.

It blends compression, saturation, and glue inside a controlled parallel engine. It turns separated drum elements into a single cohesive impact system.

Key features:

- Parallel compression architecture
- Saturation-driven drum density
- Punch and glue control
- Preserves transients while increasing weight
- Designed for modern hip-hop, trap, and cinematic drums

Best used for:

- Drum bus processing
- 808 and kick reinforcement
- Full beat glue
- Percussion energy shaping

Sound philosophy: Separate hits become one force.

### Mariana

Low-End Foundation and Sub-Pressure Control.

Mariana gives the Core Bundle a grounded foundation lane for bass sources, 808s, kick weight, and full mix translation.

It is not a loudness trick. It is the low-end authority piece that lets the rest of the core system breathe around a stable foundation.

Key features:

- Low-end authority processor
- Bass and sub foundation control
- 808, synth bass, kick, and bass-guitar support
- Pairs naturally with DrumKrushGlue
- Designed for controlled weight rather than unfocused boom

Best used for:

- Bass guitar
- 808s
- Kick weight
- Low-end groups
- Full mix foundation checks
