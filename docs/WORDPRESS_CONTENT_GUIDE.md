## Core Bundle Product Drafts

### SacredVerb

Family: FX / Spatial

Short description: Expressive harmonic reverb with emotional decay and living tail behavior.

Long description: SacredVerb is an expressive reverb system designed to create emotionally responsive space. It is tuned for musical decay behavior, harmonic movement, evolving tail structure, and clean mix integration.

Feature bullets:

- Emotion-driven reverb engine
- Harmonic decay shaping
- Smooth, musical tail evolution
- Designed for vocals, keys, pads, and melodic sources
- Clean mix integration with controlled density

Best used for:

- Vocals that need emotional depth
- Pads and ambient textures
- Cinematic sound design
- Lead instruments

Sound philosophy: Space should feel alive, not static.

### Cathedral

Family: FX / Spatial

Short description: Architectural space modeling reverb with clear reflection structure.

Long description: Cathedral is a structured reverb engine based on architectural acoustic design. It focuses on physical space logic, defined early reflections, dimensional clarity, controlled diffusion, and stable low-end response.

Feature bullets:

- Architectural reverb modeling
- Defined early reflections and spatial depth
- Clear mix placement control
- Designed for realism and width control
- Stable low-end response without wash buildup

Best used for:

- Drums in real spaces
- Orchestral instruments
- Ambient room creation
- Natural vocal placement

Sound philosophy: Space is geometry you can hear.

### DEBO

Family: Dynamics / Guard

Short description: Dynamic ceiling control and mix protection for clean output behavior.

Long description: DEBO is a mix safety engine designed to control peaks, stabilize dynamics, and enforce clean output behavior without destroying musical movement. It is a precision ceiling tool for mastering, drum bus protection, vocal peak control, and final-stage confidence.

Feature bullets:

- Transparent limiting behavior
- Controlled gain structure management
- Clean transient preservation
- Mix bus safety control
- Designed for mastering and final stages

Best used for:

- Master bus control
- Drum bus protection
- Vocal peak control
- Final mix safety layer

Sound philosophy: Control without punishment.

### DrumKrushGlue

Family: Dynamics / Guard

Short description: Parallel drum energy, saturation, punch, and rhythmic glue.

Long description: DrumKrushGlue is a rhythmic density processor designed to unify drums into a single powerful force. It blends compression, saturation, and glue inside a controlled parallel engine for drum buses, 808 and kick reinforcement, beat glue, and percussion energy shaping.

Feature bullets:

- Parallel compression architecture
- Saturation-driven drum density
- Punch and glue control
- Preserves transients while increasing weight
- Designed for modern hip-hop, trap, and cinematic drums

Best used for:

- Drum bus processing
- 808 and kick reinforcement
- Full beat glue
- Percussion energy shaping

Sound philosophy: Separate hits become one force.

### Mariana

Family: Bass / Low End

Short description: Deep low-end foundation and sub-pressure control.

Long description: Mariana is the Core Bundle foundation processor for bass sources, 808s, kick weight, and full mix translation. It gives the first public bundle a grounded low-end lane instead of making the core only space and dynamics.

Feature bullets:

- Low-end authority processor
- Bass and sub foundation control
- Pairs with DrumKrushGlue for impact and body
- Supports 808, synth bass, kick, and bass-guitar workflows
- Designed for controlled weight rather than unfocused boom

Best used for:

- Bass guitar
- 808s
- Kick weight
- Low-end groups
- Full mix foundation checks

Sound philosophy: The record needs ground before it needs height.
