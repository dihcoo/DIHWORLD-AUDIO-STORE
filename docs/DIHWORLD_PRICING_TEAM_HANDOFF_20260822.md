## Purpose

This handoff gives the DIHWorld Audio team one place to review the proposed public price ladder, the logic behind each tier, the confirmed Lifetime benefits, and the decisions that must be approved before paid checkout opens.

The prices below are proposals. They are displayed on the current storefront as proposed launch pricing and should not be treated as final until the team signs off.

## Executive Recommendation

Keep the current proposed ladder for team review. The structure gives customers a clear path from one processor to a focused family, the Core Bundle, the Complete Collection, and Lifetime membership without pricing the 42-plugin catalog like a generic utility pack.

The strongest change is presentation: show the launch price, regular comparison price, savings, included processors, and fulfillment status clearly. Do not change prices merely to imitate competitor discounts.

## Individual Processor Tier Logic

### Utility - $29 launch / $49 regular

Use only for observation, metering, or support-focused products such as SESHAT. This is the lowest public floor and should not become the default price for authored processors.

### Standard - $59 launch / $99 regular

Use for focused single-purpose processors with finished interfaces, presets, documentation, and a clear place in a session. This is the default individual tier.

### Signature - $79 launch / $129 regular

Use for processors with stronger identity, broader workflow value, or a central role in a family. Current examples: DEBO, DrumKrushGlue, Mariana, GOLD, PLATINUM, and NTRSTLLR.

### Flagship spatial - $99 launch / $149 regular

Use for SacredVerb and Cathedral. Their authored spatial systems, finished visual identity, controls, presets, and role inside the Core Bundle support the premium individual tier.

## Lifetime Membership - Confirmed Benefits

Lifetime membership should currently promise:

- The Complete 42 Collection at enrollment.
- First-look previews of DIHWorld Audio products in development.
- One Zoom technical-support session per month.
- 10% off future products.

Important: future products are discounted by 10%; they are not automatically included free unless the team later approves that benefit in writing.

## Lifetime Terms Still Requiring Team Approval

- Is the monthly Zoom session individual or group-based?
- What is the session duration, booking process, rescheduling rule, and no-show policy?
- Does unused monthly support expire, or can it roll over?
- Does "first look" mean preview videos, private demos, beta access, or all three?
- Is the 10% discount non-stackable? Which products, bundles, upgrades, or partner products are excluded?
- Is Founder Lifetime limited by date, customer count, or both?
- What are the seat count, transfer, refund, and account-sharing rules?
- What support response time is included outside the monthly Zoom session?
- What happens if a product or service is retired?

## Competitive Presentation Direction

The supplied Native Instruments, Universal Audio, and Waves screenshots consistently use large neutral sans-serif typography, generous spacing, clear product imagery, rounded calls to action, regular-price strike-throughs, and visible savings. DIHWorld should use those presentation principles while retaining its own black-and-gold identity.

Recommended storefront behavior:

- Show "Proposed launch" until the team approves pricing.
- Show launch and regular prices together.
- Use savings badges only when the comparison price is genuine and supportable.
- Keep product inclusions immediately visible.
- Do not publish an Add to Cart button until the exact installer, protected URL, terms, and refund policy are ready.
