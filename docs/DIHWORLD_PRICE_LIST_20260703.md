# DIHWorld Audio Price List

Date: 2026-07-03

Authority: current 42-plugin, 13-family DIHWorld Audio release.

Use this as the launch price sheet for WooCommerce drafts, team review, and storefront copy. These are launch-ready draft prices, not legal terms.

## Market Position

DIHWorld Audio should not race to the bottom. The suite has 42 current processors, family installers, a complete installer, Mac AU/VST3 delivery, Windows VST3 bulk delivery, and a workflow-first family system.

Useful market anchors checked on 2026-07-03:

- Soundtoys 5 lists 23 plug-ins at $599.
- iZotope Music Production Suite 9 lists 100+ pro tools at $799.
- FabFilter lists major bundles from Essentials at $419 up through Total Bundle around $949 before discount.
- Plugin Alliance subscription references sit around $149.99/year for Core and $299.99/year for Pro.
- Slate Digital Complete Access references $19.99/month or $199.99/year.

That puts DIHWorld in a strong launch lane if the complete 42-plugin collection opens at $699, with lifetime/legacy membership above it.

## Bulk / Complete Collection

Complete Collection launch price: $699.

Regular price: $1,299.

Includes the current 42-plugin suite, current family installers, the current all-family installer, manuals, current demo/support downloads, and standard update access for the purchased major version.

This price should stay below the lifetime tier. The bulk collection is a product purchase. Lifetime/legacy is a membership relationship.

## Lifetime / Legacy Membership

### Legacy Founder Lifetime

Launch price: $999.

Regular reference: $1,499.

Use for a limited founder window or first 100 customers. Includes Complete Collection, future DIHWorld Audio plugin releases in the main audio line, current manuals, current family installers, priority update notices, and legacy member pricing on future expansion products.

### Legacy Lifetime Membership

Launch price: $1,499.

Regular price: $1,999.

Use after the founder window closes. This is the standard lifetime offer for serious customers who want the whole DIHWorld Audio ecosystem.

### Legacy Studio Lifetime

Launch price: $2,499.

Regular price: $2,999.

Use for studios, production rooms, churches, schools, and teams. Draft terms: up to 5 seats, same organization, no resale, no client redistribution.

## Discount Rules

- Do not discount individual plugins below $29.
- Do not price SacredVerb + Cathedral, alone or inside an obvious spatial offer, below a $149 value floor.
- Do not discount the Complete Collection below $499 without a dated campaign note.
- Do not discount lifetime below $999.
- Keep lifetime above the Complete Collection so the membership feels premium.
- Better Buses can be free or gated at $0 as a lead magnet.
- Legacy terms should be approved before publishing: seats, transfer rules, refund window, future-product scope, support scope, and what "lifetime" means.

Set initial WooCommerce `Regular price` fields to the launch prices while the launch window is active. If sale-price columns are added later, move these launch prices to `Sale price` and use the regular prices above as `Regular price`.
