## Demo Rule

Every short demo should prove three things:

- What changed
- Why it matters
- Where the processor belongs in a real session

The repeatable clip format is:

- Dry source
- DIHWorld processed source or bus
- Full mix context
- Chain position label
- Loudness note
- Call to action

Keep most demos 45 to 90 seconds. Use 15-second social cuts only after the main proof clip exists.
